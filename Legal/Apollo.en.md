## 1. Summary

Apollo is a shared-universe fiction platform. Using it requires an account, so your account
details and the work you write are stored on our servers. This document explains what we keep,
for how long, and when it is deleted.

## 2. What we collect

| Item | Collected when | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Sign-up | Login, password reset, essential notices |
| Display name | Sign-up / edit | Shown to other users on contribution boards |
| Password | Sign-up / change | Login |
| Worlds, entries, contributions | While using the service | Providing the service |
| Access records (timestamp, action) | Login, permission changes | Statutory security obligations |

Your email address is visible only to you. Your display name is public to other users.

## 3. How passwords are stored

We never store passwords in plain text. They are kept only as `scrypt` one-way hashes, and the
hash is never included in account API responses. The operator cannot see your password.

Password reset links expire 15 minutes after they are issued, and become invalid immediately
once the password is changed.

## 4. Retention

- **Account data** — permanently deleted 30 days after you request deletion. The 30 days exist
  so an accidental deletion can be reversed; you cannot log in during this period.
- **Sessions and auth tokens** — deleted as soon as they expire.
- **Access records** — kept for one year, as required by Korean security standards for personal data.
- **Work you have written** — deleted with your account. Because this is a collaborative platform,
  parts that are merged with other authors' work may have a different deletion scope; we will
  tell you before deleting in that case.

Deletion means the data is removed, not anonymised.

## 5. Third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, and we do not use advertising or
analytics trackers.

## 6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. Display name and
password can be changed from your account page, and you can delete your own account. For anything
else, email us at the address below.

## 7. Children under 14

Apollo is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

## 8. Changes

If what we collect or how long we keep it changes, we update this document first. Changes take
effect when published, and changes that disadvantage users are announced in advance.
