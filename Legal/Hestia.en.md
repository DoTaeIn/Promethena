## 1. Summary

Hestia is a document collaboration tool built around spaces and pages. Accounts and documents are
stored on our servers, using Supabase for authentication and data storage.

## 2. What we collect

| Item | Collected when | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Sign-up | Login, invitations, essential notices |
| Google account data (email, name, avatar) | When using Google sign-in | Login |
| Password | Email sign-up | Login |
| Documents and comments | While using the service | Providing the service |
| Uploaded images | When you attach them | Displaying in documents |
| Notification records | On mentions and shares | Delivering notifications |
| Access records | Login, permission changes | Statutory security obligations |

## 3. Processors

We use **Supabase** for authentication, database, and file storage, and **Vercel** for hosting and
delivery. If you use Google sign-in, the items above are received from Google.

## 4. Workspaces and visibility

Documents are visible to members of the workspace they belong to. Creating a share link makes the
document viewable by anyone who has the link, so please check the scope before sharing.

## 5. Retention

- **Account data** — permanently deleted 30 days after you request deletion.
- **Sessions and auth tokens** — deleted as soon as they expire.
- **Access records** — kept for one year.
- **Documents, images, notifications** — deleted with your account. Documents edited by several
  people remain in the workspace, with your authorship attribution removed.

## 6. Third parties

We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising. We do not read your documents or
use them as training data.

## 7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time, either from your
account page or by emailing us.

## 8. Children under 14

Hestia is not directed at children under 14.

## 9. Changes

If what we collect or which processors we use changes, this document is updated first and takes
effect when published.
